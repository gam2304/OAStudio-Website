Defining the Silent Modernity.

OA Architecture & Design is a multidisciplinary studio founded on the principles of architectural silence—where precision meets permanence. Led by Oussama Attia, we bridge the gap between rigorous technical BIM execution and visionary aesthetic composition.

Founder Spotlight

Oussama Attia

An ENAU graduate with a distinguished academic record, Oussama Attia has cultivated a practice that prioritizes the structural integrity of a concept over decorative trends.

His expertise lies in the integration of Building Information Modeling (BIM) and Artificial Intelligence within the architectural workflow, ensuring that every project is not only visually arresting but technically flawless and future-proof.

With an international portfolio spanning luxury residential, commercial monoliths, and cultural installations, Oussama brings a global perspective to local landscapes, advocating for a design language that speaks through volume and light.
