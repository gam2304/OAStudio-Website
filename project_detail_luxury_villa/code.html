The Obsidian Villa

keyboard_double_arrow_down

THE NARRATIVE

Geneva, Switzerland

"An exercise in the silent dialogue between raw stone and the weightless clarity of light."

Designed as a private sanctuary for a prominent art collector, the Obsidian Villa redefines the relationship between structural mass and natural landscape. The project began as an interrogation of the site’s volcanic topography, resulting in a series of interlocking volumes that appear to emerge directly from the earth.

Every axis was meticulously calculated to capture the shifting solar patterns, ensuring that the interior spaces are bathed in a controlled, rhythmic play of shadows. The palette is intentionally restrictive: charcoal concrete, blackened steel, and ancient travertine.
